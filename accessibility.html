Accessibility

Last updated: 25 November 2026

The goal is that anyone can browse the list, including with a keyboard, a screen reader or larger text. The site aims for WCAG 2.1 level AA.

What the site does

  • An accessibility menu on every page (the button in the corner): larger text, high contrast, underlined links, stop animations and a plainer font. Settings stay in your browser.
  • Text contrast of at least 4.5:1 against its background, checked with a measuring tool on every text element.
  • Full keyboard use with a visible focus ring, and a "skip to the games" link at the top.
  • Images of games have text alternatives with the game's name.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, animations are switched off.
  • Right-to-left layout for Hebrew and Arabic.

Known limits

  • Game descriptions in languages other than English are machine-translated and may be imperfect.
  • Game icons come from the games' creators; text inside those images cannot be read by screen readers.

Found a problem?

Tell us what did not work and on which device, and it will be fixed: open an issue on the site's GitHub page.
